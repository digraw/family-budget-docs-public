1. Download & Installation

The native Android app is distributed directly via authenticated secure APK downloads.

  1. Log into your account on your Android phone browser at family-budget.getdigraw.com.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Download Android App (or access /api/v1/app/download).
  3. Download the signed release APK (e.g. family-budget-tracker-v1.0.79.apk).
  4. Tap the downloaded file to install. If prompted by Android, allow "Install from this source".
  5. Open Family Budget and log in with your email or Google account.

2. Biometric App Lock (Fingerprint & Face ID)

Protect your financial privacy whenever you hand your phone to someone else.

  1. In the mobile app, tap Settings in the bottom navigation.
  2. Toggle "Enable Biometric Lock".
  3. Confirm with your fingerprint or face scan.
  4. Security Coverage: Biometric authentication is enforced both on initial app launch and whenever the app resumes from the background.

3. Real-Time Bank Push Notification Ingestion

Never manually type a coffee or grocery receipt again. The mobile app can capture transaction push notifications sent by your banking apps in real time.

🔒 Hybrid Privacy Filter:
The on-device listener checks notifications against whitelisted banking package IDs (e.g. Barclays, Monzo, Revolut, Google Wallet) OR matches currency symbols (`£`, `$`, `€`, `₹`) + financial verbs (*spent*, *paid*, *debited*) with your active account names. All personal messages (WhatsApp, SMS, cameras) are strictly ignored on-device.

How to Enable:

  1. Go to Settings → Notification Listener in the mobile app.
  2. Tap "Grant Notification Access" to open Android system settings and toggle permission on for Family Budget Tracker.
  3. Whenever you tap your card or pay with Google Wallet, the app captures the alert, parses the amount and merchant, categorizes it, and adds it to your ledger with zero delay.

4. Interactive Skia Charts

Built with Shopify React Native Skia for 60fps hardware-accelerated fluid touch interactions:

  • Cash Flow Sparklines: Real-time mini sparklines in the dashboard header.
  • Radial Category Donut: Smooth touch-enabled category breakdowns.
  • Multi-Cycle Spending Trend Lines: Touch and scrub across historical pay cycles.
  • Interactive Mortgage Amortization: Scrub along your loan repayment curve.

5. Hands-Free Voice Commands

Tap the floating microphone on any mobile screen to dictate transactions:

  • "Paid £45 for petrol on Barclaycard"
  • "Received £250 freelance payment in Chase"
  • "Quick fund £50 to Holiday goal"

6. In-App Updates

Whenever a new version of the app is released, you will receive an in-app banner and push notification with release notes and a 1-tap download link to upgrade cleanly without losing any data.
