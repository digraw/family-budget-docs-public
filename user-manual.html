1. Financial Dashboard & "Safe to Spend"

Your household command center displaying live spendable liquidity, income burn velocity, connected accounts, and category metrics.

💻 React Web App
📱 Native Mobile (Live Flow)

What It Does

  • Displays the central Safe to Spend (S2S) gauge using the current account, bill, budget, and goal data for the selected pay cycle.
  • Quick-stat overview cards: Liquid Cash Balance, Inflow Income, Expense Burn, and Allocated Savings.
  • Instant family filter chip (👥 All, 👤 Sarah, 👤 John) to toggle between household and individual views without layout shifts.

How It Works Under the Hood

Dynamic Pay Cycle Formula:
Safe to Spend = Liquid Cash Balance − Committed Bills − Active Budget Limits − Physical Goal Reserves

Budgets align to your actual salary dates rather than calendar months. Tapping "Live Math" breaks down the exact arithmetic and contributing accounts.

How to Configure & Use

  1. Go to Settings → Pay Cycle Settings to set your salary schedule (Monthly, Bi-weekly, or Specific Day).
  2. Click or tap the Family Filter Chip to switch between personal and combined household totals.
  3. Use the Next / Prev buttons in the header to inspect previous or projected future cycles.

🎙️ Voice Commands & Accessibility (Add, Remove, Filter)

Both Desktop Web and Native Mobile provide deterministic voice transaction actions. Tap the Voice microphone button on the transactions screen to speak:

  • Add Commands (e.g. Add £20 Costa Coffee or Add £60 grocery Sainsburys to Barclays debit on 27 July): Automatically infers today's date, your primary checking account, and categorizes via merchant learning. Pre-populates the Add modal for visual review before saving.
  • Remove Commands (e.g. Remove £1 Counter Credit or Remove £81.50 Sainsbury on 20 August): Requires exact amount/currency and date or merchant.
    • Single Match: Instantly opens the transaction modal for confirmation.
    • Multiple Matches: Displays a "Choose transaction (N matches)" disambiguation dialog showing dates, accounts, and amounts so you can pick the exact one. Voice never deletes automatically without confirmation.
  • Filter Commands (e.g. Filter today's transactions, Filter source notification, Filter description Sainsbury category groceries July £50): Instantly applies multi-dimensional filters directly to the live transaction ledger and scrolls to the first matching row, with a 1-tap "Clear voice filters" button.

7. Investment Portfolio & Ticker Sync

Multi-broker portfolio tracking across Vanguard, AJ Bell, and international investment platforms with automated market pricing.

💻 React Web App
📱 Native Mobile (Live Flow)

What It Does

  • Holdings Tab: Individual funds, shares, unit costs, live market prices, and current valuations.
  • Performance Tab: Interactive quarterly portfolio valuation growth curve and time-weighted return tracking.
  • Insights Tab: Asset class distribution (Equities, Bonds, Cash) and geographic market exposure.

Multi-Currency Multiples & Valuation Rationale

The investment engine normalizes supported quote denominations and currencies before calculating current values:

  • Minor-to-Major Currency Scaling Multiples (Pence to Pounds / GBX → GBP): Many UK equities, funds, and investment trusts trade and quote on the London Stock Exchange in pence (GBp / GBX) rather than pounds. The pricing engine automatically detects pence denominations and applies a decimal scaling multiple of ÷ 100 (0.01x).
    Rationale: Without the decimal scaling multiple, a holding of 1,000 units in a fund quoted at 450 GBp (£4.50) would naively compute to £450,000 instead of £4,500, creating an erroneous 100× net worth distortion.
  • Cross-Currency FX Multipliers: For global holdings denominated in foreign currencies (e.g. US equities in USD, European index trackers in EUR), real-time foreign exchange conversion multiples convert asset market values into your family's base currency.
  • Fractional units: Fractional quantities are multiplied by normalized quotes and rounded for display in the portfolio currency.

How It Works Under the Hood

A user-triggered refresh obtains available public market quotes and FX rates, with a three-hour cache. Unsupported, private, or ambiguous holdings keep their latest verified statement value. Statements remain the reconciliation source of truth.

How to Configure & Use

  1. Open Investments from the navigation menu.
  2. Switch between Holdings, Performance, and Insights tabs.
  3. Click or tap "Refresh Prices" to request available market quotes and currency conversion.

9. Zero-Based Envelope Budgeting & Spending Ceilings

Transform your household finances from passive tracking to proactive cash allocation. Give every available pound a specific job before it is spent with cash-backed envelopes, automated credit card reservations, and multi-cycle target scheduling.

💻 React Web App (Budget Hub)
📱 Native Mobile (Live Flow)

1. 🧭 The Product Model: "Track Only" vs "Envelope Budgeting"

Family Budget Tracker supports two distinct budgeting workflows, allowing you to choose the exact level of financial structure you need:

  • Track Only Mode (Default): Pure transaction and cash-flow tracking. You monitor your central Safe to Spend gauge, track recurring bills, and set lightweight Category Budget Limits as spending ceilings without having to assign every pound.
  • Zero-Based Envelope Planning (Active): A complete cash-backed envelope accounting system. Every pound currently in your checking and liquid cash accounts is explicitly assigned to an envelope until Ready to Assign is exactly £0.00.
📐 The Core Zero-Based Accounting Invariant:
Confirmed Budgeting Cash = Ready to Assign + ∑ (All Envelope Available Balances)

Envelope Available = Carry-in + Assigned + Transaction Activity + Moved In − Moved Out

Note: "Zero-Based" does not mean your bank account balance is zero or that you should spend all your money. It means zero money remains unassigned without a designated purpose.

2. 📂 The 5 Envelope Group Types

Envelopes are organized into 5 purpose-built functional groups:

  • 💳 Everyday Spending: Variable day-to-day lifestyle categories (Groceries, Dining Out, Fuel, Entertainment, Shopping). Categorized transactions create real-time debit activity.
  • 🗓️ Annual & Upcoming Bills: Scheduled or irregular lump-sum commitments (Car Insurance, Road Tax, MOT, Boiler Service, School Fees, Christmas, Holiday Season). Multi-cycle targets break massive annual bills into manageable pay-cycle assignments.
  • 🎯 Savings Goals: Long-term cash reservations (Emergency Fund, House Deposit, Wedding, Future Purchases). Money remains safely protected in your budgeting accounts or physical savings accounts.
  • 💳 Credit Card Payment Envelopes: System-managed envelopes that automatically reserve checking cash to pay off credit card balances as purchases occur.
  • 🛡️ Buffers: Dedicated safety reserves (Emergency Buffer, Next Cycle Pay Buffer) ensuring you never accidentally dip into next month's funds.

3. 🎯 5 Intelligent Target Scheduling Types

Targets provide smart funding suggestions each pay cycle without creating fake transactions or locking money involuntarily:

  • 1. Set aside per cycle: Suggests assigning a fixed, recurring amount every pay cycle (e.g. £150 every cycle for Groceries).
  • 2. Refill to amount: Smart top-up target that only requests enough money to restore the envelope back to a target ceiling (e.g. Refill Dining Out to £200—if £70 remained from last cycle, it only asks for £130).
  • 3. Have amount by date: Calculates the exact amount required across remaining pay cycles before a target deadline:
    Required per Cycle = max(0, Target Amount − Available Amount) ÷ Remaining Pay Cycles
    Example: £1,200 Car Insurance due in 6 cycles suggests exactly £200 per cycle.
  • 4. Recurring due date: Automatically rolls forward to the next annual or monthly period once funded or spent (e.g. Annual MOT every August).
  • 5. No target: Flexible manual envelope funded on an ad-hoc basis.

4. 💳 Credit Card Automatic Cash Reservation Engine

Family Budget Tracker solves credit card debt by keeping credit purchases 100% cash-backed in real time:

  1. Funded Card Purchase: When you buy £60 of Groceries with your credit card, £60 is deducted from your Groceries envelope and automatically moved to your Credit Card Payment envelope.
  2. Cash Integrity: Your checking account cash remains untouched until you actually pay the credit card bill.
  3. Paying the Card: Making a credit card payment is recorded as an internal transfer from checking to your credit card account. This simultaneously reduces your Card Payment envelope availability and credit card liability—without recording a second duplicate expense.
  4. Unfunded Overspending Warning: If you spend on a credit card in a category with insufficient funds, the envelope turns red and flags unfunded debt, prompting you to reassign cash to cover the balance.

5. 🔄 Overspending, Rollover & Negative Balances

  • Positive Balances: Any unspent funds in an envelope automatically roll over into the next pay cycle as positive carry-in available funds.
  • Cash Overspending: If you overspend an envelope from a checking account, the negative balance directly reduces your Ready to Assign pool in the subsequent cycle, ensuring your budget remains mathematically sound.
  • Quick Money Movement: Tap "Move Money" on any envelope to shift available funds between categories with 1 tap and 0 bank friction.

6. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Shared Family Envelopes & Many-to-One Category Mappings

For households utilizing Premium Family sharing, the envelope budgeting engine provides unified clarity without administrative headache:

  • One Shared Family Scope per Currency: The entire household shares a single unified Ready to Assign and envelope availability pool per currency.
  • Many-to-One Category Mappings: Multiple family members' personal categories (e.g. Sarah's Groceries and John's Groceries) seamlessly map into a single shared household Groceries envelope. Transactions preserve individual user ownership while ledger activity reconciles to the family envelope.
  • Family Member Lens: Switch between 👥 All Members or an individual member to inspect activity. The filter does not change Ready to Assign, Assigned, Available, targets, suggestions, or cycle balances.
  • Role-Based Allocation Permissions: Owners and admins manage setup, migration, envelopes, targets, and lifecycle. Members may assign, unassign, and move money. Viewers are read-only.

7. ⚡ Controlled 1-Click Migration & Reversible Switching

  • 1-Click Migration Preview: If you have existing Category Budget Limits and Savings Goals, the app generates a deterministic migration preview that converts limits into envelope targets and reserves without writing a single permanent row until you confirm.
  • Reversible Pause & Resume: You can pause Envelope Budgeting at any time to return to lightweight Track Only mode. Pausing preserves all allocations, targets, and history intact, allowing you to resume whenever you are ready.
