✉️ Zero-Based Envelope Budgeting

What is Zero-Based Envelope Budgeting?

Zero-based budgeting is a proactive financial methodology where every pound of confirmed cash is assigned a specific job (e.g. Groceries, Car Insurance, Holiday Savings, Buffer) before you spend it. The goal is to reach Ready to Assign = £0.00. It does not mean your bank account balance is zero; it means 100% of your money has an intended purpose so none is lost to impulse spending.

How does it differ from "Safe to Spend" and "Budget Limits"?

Family Budget Tracker supports two distinct modes:

  • Track Only Mode: You track transactions against a central Safe to Spend gauge and set passive Category Budget Limits as spending ceilings.
  • Active Envelope Budgeting: You actively allocate actual liquid cash into cash-backed envelopes with automated credit-card reservations, multi-cycle target schedules, and cycle carry-over rules.

How does credit card spending work with envelopes?

When you spend £50 on Groceries using a credit card, £50 is deducted from your Groceries envelope and automatically moved to your Credit Card Payment envelope. Your checking cash is preserved until you pay the card bill. Paying the credit card is recorded as an internal transfer between checking and card, which reduces the Card Payment envelope reserve and your card debt simultaneously without recording a duplicate expense.

How do annual expenses (e.g. Car Insurance, Christmas) work?

Instead of facing sudden large lump sums, you create an "Have amount by date" target. The app calculates your remaining pay cycles and suggests a smooth, equal assignment each cycle (e.g. £1,200 Car Insurance due in 6 cycles suggests £200 per cycle).

Can I switch between Track Only and Envelope Budgeting?

Yes. You can pause Envelope Budgeting at any time from Settings or the Budget page. Pausing instantly restores your Category Budget Limits view while securely preserving all envelope balances, targets, and historical allocation records so you can resume whenever you want.

How does family sharing work with envelopes?

Under Premium Family, your household shares a single unified Ready to Assign pool per currency. Multiple family members' personal categories (e.g. Sarah's Groceries and John's Groceries) map into one shared household Groceries envelope. You can use the family filter to view individual member activity breakdowns while total household envelope reserves remain synchronized.

🏡 Mortgages & Investments

How does the mortgage interest rate solver work?

When you link bank repayments to a mortgage account, the system uses dynamic amortization. If your monthly debit payment changes due to a rate adjustment, our binary search solver calculates the exact new annual interest rate from your balance, remaining term, and payment amount.

How are investment and pension returns calculated?

Pensions derive return performance directly from total cumulative net contributions versus current statement valuation truth, avoiding false performance distortions.
